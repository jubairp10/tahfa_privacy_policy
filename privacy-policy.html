Who we are

Tahfa is operated by Code Fifty One Pvt Ltd ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at store@code51.in.

What we collect and why

Your account

You can read the Quran, see prayer times and use most features as a guest. An account is only needed for things tied to you, such as adding a Tahfa device. When you register or sign in, we collect:

  • your username and password (used to sign you in);
  • your phone number and country code;
  • an email address, if you add one to your profile.

When you sign in, our servers issue sign-in tokens that the app keeps on your phone so you stay signed in.

Location

If you allow location access, Tahfa uses your phone's location to:

  • calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction — this is done on your phone;
  • show your city name, using your phone's built-in location lookup (provided by Apple or Google);
  • find mosques near you — your coordinates are sent to our servers and to Google Maps (see Services we rely on).

The last known location is stored on your phone so the app works quickly. If you don't allow location, Tahfa uses a default location (Makkah) and prayer times will not match your area.

Your Tahfa device

When you add a Tahfa Box to your account, we store information needed to run it: its device ID and name, the mosque you choose for it, its volume and tuning settings, whether it is online, and the name of the Wi-Fi network it is connected to.

To connect the Box to your Wi-Fi, the app uses Bluetooth to find nearby Tahfa devices and sends your Wi-Fi network name and password directly to the Box. The app does not send your Wi-Fi password to our servers.

Camera

The camera turns on only when you choose to scan a Tahfa device's QR code or open the augmented-reality (AR) Qibla view. The picture is shown live on your screen and processed on your phone. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded, and no face or body information is collected. See AR Qibla view.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) gives your phone a notification token so messages can be delivered to it.

Stored only on your phone

These stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Quran bookmarks and your last-read position;
  • your chosen reciter, display options and app language;
  • prayer-time notification choices and calculation method;
  • copies of translated app text, kept so it loads faster.

Uninstalling the app removes them.

Services we rely on

Some features work by contacting other services. Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address and basic device information to the provider.

  • Google Firebase — notifications (Cloud Messaging) and app integrity checks (App Check), which confirm requests come from the genuine app.
  • Google Maps Platform — finding and mapping nearby mosques. Your location is sent to Google for this. Directions open in Google Maps.
  • Google Translate — if you choose a language other than English, the app's own interface text is sent to Google Translate. Your personal information is not included.
  • Google Fonts — some typefaces, including the Arabic calligraphy font, are downloaded from Google.
  • Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network) — Quran recitation audio is streamed from this service, which sees which verses are requested.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — checking whether a newer version of the app is available.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your personal data, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed above, to run the features you use;
  • with hosting and infrastructure providers that run our servers for us — Amazon Web Services (AWS), in its Mumbai, India region;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the public;
  • as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and device information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete this information within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices and rights

  • Use Tahfa as a guest. No account is needed to read the Quran, see prayer times or use the Qibla compass.
  • Control permissions. You can turn off location, camera, Bluetooth and notification access at any time in your phone's settings. The features that need them will stop working.
  • Delete your account in the app, under Profile → Delete Account.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing store@code51.in.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as objecting to how we use your data or complaining to your local data-protection authority.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Passwords are hidden as you type and never shown in the app, and sign-in tokens are stored on your phone only. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to fix problems quickly.

AR Qibla view

The Qibla compass has an optional AR view that shows the Qibla direction over your camera's live picture.

  • It opens only when you tap AR on the Qibla screen, and the camera switches off as soon as you leave that view.
  • The camera picture stays on your phone. It is not recorded, saved, uploaded or shared, and it is not used to identify people or places.
  • It uses your location and compass only to point toward the Qibla.
  • Young users should open the AR view with a parent or guardian. Using the camera means holding up the phone and moving around, so an adult should supervise and make sure the surroundings are safe.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
